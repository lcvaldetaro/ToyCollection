Gepetto Toy Database Manager is a complete collection management application for collectors and hobbyists. The application helps you catalog, organize, and maintain your collections with detailed records and photos.

Use the application to track slot cars, model trains, static models, model kits, and custom collections.

Please share feedback or suggestions through the Play Store link. Desktop versions for macOS and Windows are available at https://gepetto.club.

Key Features & Capabilities:

• Multi-Category Organization:
- Built-in categories include Slot Cars, Model Trains, Static Models, Model Kits, and Others.
- Create and manage custom categories with custom icons, titles, and photo prefix codes.
- View total inventory counts and collection summaries on the main dashboard.

• Comprehensive Item Records:
- Record up to 34 detailed attributes for each collectible item.
- Track reference numbers, model names, manufacturers, scales, and production years.
- Record mechanical parts, chassis details, body types, and motor specifications.
- Maintain acquisition history, purchase prices, current market values, and sales data.
- Track item condition, packaging status, reproduction status, and personal notes.

• Manufacturer Directory:
- Maintain a complete directory of manufacturers with countries of origin and historical notes.
- Attach manufacturer logos and factory images.
- Rename manufacturers safely with automatic updates across all associated item records.

• Photo & Media Management:
- Attach primary photos and secondary image galleries to any collectible item.
- Resolve photos automatically with smart filename prefixes and reference numbers.
- View high-resolution photos in an integrated viewer.

• Full Backup & Restore:
- Export your entire collection into a single compressed ZIP archive.
- Backup files include all database records, custom categories, manufacturers, and photos.
- Restore your collection on the same device or transfer data to a new device.
- Stream large archives safely without excessive device memory usage.

• Cloud & Network Synchronization:
- Synchronize your collection data and photos across phones, tablets, and computers.
- Connect directly to your private SFTP server with secure authentication.
- Perform smart differential synchronization that transfers only new or updated files.
- Synchronize data from remote web servers with automatic hash verification.

• Website Catalog Generation:
- Generate complete static HTML web pages directly from your collection database.
- Create category index pages, manufacturer galleries, and individual item detail pages.
- Publish the generated files to your web server to share your collection online.

• Free, Registration-Free & Privacy-Focused:
- No user accounts, registration, or sign-ins required.
- No advertisements and no analytics tracking.
- All your collection data stays on your local device and your private storage.
