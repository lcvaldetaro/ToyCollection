# How to Start Your Own Collection

When you first install this application, it connects to Gepetto's Toy Collection.
Follow these steps to disconnect from Gepetto's collection and manage your own personal collection.

---

### Step 1: Reset the Database to Defaults
1. Select **Settings** (gear icon) in the navigation bar.
2. Open the **Backup & Restore** tab.
3. Scroll down to the **Danger Zone** section.
4. Click **Reset Database to Defaults**.
5. Confirm the action in the dialog.

**Result**:
- All toys in Gepetto's collection are removed (0 toys).
- All 198 standard manufacturers and their logos are kept in your database.
- The web synchronization URL is disconnected so the app will not download Gepetto's toys again.
- The collection title changes to **"My Toy Collection"**.

---

### Step 2: Configure Your Data Folder (Desktop Only)
- **Desktop**: In **Settings**, open the **General** tab and select your local **Data Folder**. Your toy photos and backup files are saved in this folder.
- **Android**: The application manages data storage automatically. You do not need to configure a folder.

---

### Step 3: Customize Categories and Manufacturers
- Open **Settings** -> **Categories** to edit, add, or remove toy categories.
- Open **Manufacturers** in the navigation bar to add manufacturers for your models.

---

### Step 4: Add Your Toys
1. Open **Dashboard** or any category page.
2. Click the **+** (Add Toy) button.
3. Enter your toy description, manufacturer, scale, condition, and purchase details.
4. Add pictures of your models.

<!-- !web -->
---

### Step 5: Save Regular Backups
1. Open **Settings** -> **Backup & Restore**.
2. Click **Back Up Collection** to save a .zip archive with all your toys, manufacturers, and photos.
