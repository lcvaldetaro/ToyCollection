Organize your toys into custom groups.

### Managing Categories
- **Add Category**: Tap **Add Category** to create a new category (such as Model Kits, Diecast, Trains, or Slot Cars).
- **Display Title**: The title shown on the category screen and navigation menu.
- **Icon**: Choose a visual symbol for the category in the navigation bar.
- **Photo Prefix**: A short prefix code (for example `kit_` or `car_`) to identify photos for this category.
- **Edit & Delete**: Change details at any time, or delete categories you no longer need.
