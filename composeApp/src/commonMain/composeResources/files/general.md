### Features
- **Theme**: Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System Default** to adjust screen colors.
- **Language**: Choose your display language (**System Default**, **English**, **Português**, **Français**, **Español**, **Italiano**, or **Deutsch**). The user interface updates immediately.
- **Collection Title**: Change the custom name shown at the top of your screen.
<!-- desktop -->
- **Data Folder**: Choose the folder on your computer where your photos and files are stored.
