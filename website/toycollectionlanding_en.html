Simple & Systematic Organization

Cataloging and preserving your collection takes just 3 easy steps.

1

Set Up Categories & Manufacturers

Start with built-in categories (Slot Cars, Model Trains, Static Models, Model Kits, Others) or create your own custom categories with dedicated photo prefix rules. Add manufacturers along with their country of origin and history.

2

Catalog Collectibles with Full Details

Add new toys with reference numbers, model names, and manufacturers. Fill in up to 34 technical and historical attributes including scales, motor types, condition ratings, purchase prices, and attach high-resolution photos.

3

Explore, Backup & Synchronize

Analyze collection metrics on your visual dashboard. Safeguard your entire collection in a single compressed ZIP file, or sync seamlessly to your private SFTP server to access your catalog on phone, tablet, and computer.
